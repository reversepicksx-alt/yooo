Reverse Picks engine rules specification: Treat Reverse Picks production math as the immutable quantitative source of truth; use external evidence and AI only to explain, qualify, or identify missing information, never to overwrite a projection, probability, recommendation, settlement result, or saved-pick snapshot. Every request must first establish canonical identity: sport, player identity and provider ID, player team and provider team ID, opponent and provider opponent ID, league and season, fixture ID, fixture date/status, and player venue as home or away; fixture team IDs are authoritative for orientation, a player cannot be treated as home or away from a user label, legacy neutral venues must be resolved to home/away before odds, possession, history, or narrative logic, and stale or finished fixtures must never be used as the next-match fallback. If identity, fixture, team, opponent, date, or provider evidence is ambiguous, contradictory, unavailable, quota-blocked, or not from the same fixture, return UNKNOWN or defer rather than guess. Use API-Football as the authoritative source for soccer fixture identity, player/team context, player stats, prediction inputs, and settlement; use SportsGameOdds PrizePicks lines only as market-reference context and preserve timestamped line movement without allowing it to replace Reverse Picks math; keep provider credentials server-side. Normalize every sport response to the unified contract containing playerName, playerId, teamName, opponentName, sport, propType, line, projection, pOver, pUnder, recommendation, confidenceScore, confidenceLevel, gameLogs with value/opponent/venue/score/date, matchupOverview, sharpSummary, reasoning, tacticalBreakdown, and risk signals; render or explain features from data presence, not sport-name UI gates. For player props, map the requested market to the exact provider settlement field and canonicalize aliases that share a field; never treat an unavailable field, empty response, null value, or fallback estimate as a measured zero, and never mix player history, team meetings, current-fixture context, or different venue samples as if they were equivalent evidence. Build the baseline from verified historical game logs and applicable season, venue, opponent, role, position, league, competition, and workload evidence; deduplicate logs by fixture identity, require actual player minutes for player-history and H2H appearances, use same-role and same-venue cohorts where required, broaden seasons or competitions only as an explicit auditable fallback, and disclose thin or unavailable samples. Apply the Bayesian projection in layers: establish a prior from the correct player/role/position/league context, combine verified observations with sample-size-aware shrinkage, incorporate venue and matchup evidence only when provenance and coverage are valid, model uncertainty with an appropriate standard deviation rather than squaring raw variance twice, calculate over and under probabilities against the saved/requested line, and preserve both probabilities with pOver plus pUnder approximately summing to 100. Use possession, opponent allowance, team schedule, lineup, formation, tactical role, match script, pressure, odds, congestion, injuries, red-card risk, and other contextual signals only when explicitly available and correctly sourced; derived or estimated possession must be labeled fallback/unknown rather than verified match possession, shadow signals remain explanatory until leakage-safe settled-pick replay validates a live multiplier, and correlated signals must not be double-counted. Resolve exact positions from confirmed lineup grids and formations when available, keep generic positions incomplete, keep goalkeeper evidence goalkeeper-only, and keep tactical explanations role-specific and prop-specific without unsupported claims. Apply deterministic calibration and safety controls after projection: use direction-aware and hierarchical empirical calibration with shrinkage so thin buckets borrow strength instead of overfitting, use odds-tier, venue, position, role, league, prop, and recommendation buckets only with sufficient samples, cap confidence when evidence quality or conviction is low, suppress misleading HIGH confidence when independent prop-safety or line-deviation evidence signals risk, treat AVOID/PASS as avoidance calibration rather than normal wager ROI, keep soccer hard blocks such as clearances OVER and shots OVER safeguards, and never allow an AI narrative or market feed to bypass these caps. Confidence describes the final calibrated evidence-backed direction, not the amount of prose; projection, probability, recommendation, confidence, line, and factor ledger must remain internally consistent, and any final Bayesian direction change must reconcile the narrative rather than erase valid evidence or downgrade to unexplained math-only output. Snapshot final inputs, factor ledger, model version, fingerprint, evidence provenance, unavailable-data reasons, and audit metadata at the prediction boundary so analysis and saved history can be reproduced; analysis owns the complete narrative and recent-game evidence while compact cards remain concise. AI must explain the exact final projection ledger and its cache identity, must reject provider error strings as data, must not invent citations, lineups, roles, possession, injuries, odds, history, or stats, and must state uncertainty and rerun requirements; audit modules are observational and cannot alter production Reverse Picks math. Live tracking may show saved PROJ separately from NOW and PACE, must use the persisted exact fixture ID, prioritize active fixtures, and warn on live pace or hit-rate divergence rather than silently rewriting the pre-match projection. Settlement is terminal only after exact fixture, exact player, correct provider stat field, finished status, and source provenance are verified; never settle halftime or incomplete matches, never convert unavailable/null stats into zero, let stat evidence override misleading minutes fields when the player clearly has recorded activity, defer when exact stats are missing, mark genuine non-participation as DNP only after the finished-fixture player/lineup evidence supports it, allow only HIT or MISS for half-line props, keep PUSH out of .5-line outcomes, and wire settlement notifications through every live and background write path. Calibration may use only settled, deduplicated, inclusion-valid ledger rows with explicit sample counts and truncation warnings; prediction failures and optional persistence failures must return structured retryable or write-skipped states rather than opaque errors, and analytics/cache quota failures must not make a valid prediction fail. Preserve canonical saved/requested lines across prediction, line intel, asynchronous analysis, saved analysis, and factor ledgers; preserve deliberate saved-pick revisions as fresh snapshots; invalidate deleted-pick caches; do not expose owner-only media or provider dashboard details to ordinary users; and when a required fact is absent, the correct output is UNKNOWN, unavailable, deferred, or retryable—not an invented value.
