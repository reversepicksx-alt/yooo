---
name: Causal corroboration threshold
description: Confidence and production-flip rule for exact-role causal evidence.
---

Three clean exact-role cohort samples can support a PASS or a provisional causal confirm/contradiction, but cannot alone be strong confidence. A production direction flip needs at least five clean exact-role samples, or three clean samples plus aligned current-regime, matching-venue, or verified player H2H evidence.

**Why:** A three-row cohort can identify a plausible mechanism but is too thin to be labeled strong without independent agreement.

**How to apply:** Persist the aligned corroboration sources and production-flip eligibility in every causal packet. Cap subscriber confidence below strong whenever clean exact-role sample size is under five, including when three rows have sufficient corroboration to make a flip eligible.
