---
name: Tactical evidence provenance
description: Role, player-opponent history, and same-position cohort evidence must remain provider-grounded and sample-labeled.
---

Observed lineup positions and positive-minutes fixture records are the strongest tactical role evidence. Generic attacker labels must not default to Pressing Forward; wide creator output can support an Inverted Winger/creator interpretation. Player-vs-opponent hit rates and same-position cohort averages must expose their exact sample size and mark thin cohorts rather than padding or overstating them. Same-role cohorts are evidence-only, carry explicit verifies/contradicts/neutral/unavailable verdicts, and may include cross-prop averages without changing the projection.

**Why:** Generic cached roles produced raw-number explanations and could misdescribe creator profiles. Team meetings and bench rows also made opponent evidence look stronger than the player-level record justified.

**How to apply:** Persist role source/evidence, exact-opponent appearance counts, cohort status, and final-direction verdict with every prediction. Keep the packet explanation-only until settled-pick replay validates any numeric use.

Side-specific labels such as LB/RB are valid only when the fixture row or a grounded/manual profile confirms that exact position. A broad provider category such as D/DEF may participate in broad evidence, but must remain “position not verified” and must not enter a side-specific cohort.

**Why:** Provider category fallbacks can be stored as plausible exact positions and were previously capable of making an unverified defender appear to be a confirmed left back in customer-facing evidence.

**How to apply:** Carry positionVerified and positionSource with cohort rows; filter exact-position cohorts to fixture-observed or grounded/manual exact matches, and render full player/team/stat/possession labels without collapsing provenance into a short code.
