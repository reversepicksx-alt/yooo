name: Tactical explanation assembly
description: Customer-facing tactical narratives must be generated once from final model state with role-specific mechanism precedence.
---

Generate the customer-facing tactical explanation only after the final projection, direction, confidence controls, and tactical packet are complete. Do not append an early narrative to a later narrative; this creates duplicate role/shape claims and can preserve stale direction language. Prefer the specific tactical role mechanism before the broad position fallback (for example, fullback before generic defender).

**Why:** The Fofana analysis previously repeated role and formation evidence, described a role-specific player with a broad-position sentence, and made the Under look contradictory when recent form was Hot. The raw model data was valid, but the stitched explanation damaged trust.

**How to apply:** Keep one canonical tactical context section, reconcile the final ledger before rendering it, state why the recommendation survives conflicting signals, and label thin or unavailable evidence explicitly.
