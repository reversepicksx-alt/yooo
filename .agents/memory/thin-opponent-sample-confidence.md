---
name: Thin opponent sample confidence
description: Opponent-specific soccer prop evidence can be misleading when based on only one or two prior matchups.
---

Opponent-specific history with fewer than three comparable meetings should not support very-high confidence by itself. Keep the broad calibration layer, but cap the displayed confidence conservatively and disclose the thin sample. A large possession or game-state swing can still defeat a valid historical projection, so do not retroactively rewrite genuine misses as settlement errors.

**Why:** A pair of Liga MX pass predictions showed that thin opponent samples and unexpected possession dominance can create severe overconfidence even when fixture, lineup, and final stats are correct.

**How to apply:** When opponent-specific sample size is 1–2, cap confidence at 72% and add a tactical alert. Treat positive provider stats as participation evidence during settlement, regardless of stale minutes fields.
