Here’s exactly how to teach your bot this skill using API-Football data.
Core Concept
You need the bot to classify every player into one of three pressure profiles:
•  Pressure Sensitive (“Freezers”) → Pass volume drops sharply when the team has low possession or faces high-pressing opponents.
•  Pressure Neutral → Pass volume stays relatively stable.
•  Pressure Resistant / Thrives → Pass volume stays high or actually increases under pressure.
How to Build It with API-Football Data
API-Football gives you per-game:
•  Passes attempted / completed
•  Team possession %
•  Opponent (so you can flag high-pressing teams)
•  Minutes played
Step-by-step logic to code:
1. Create a Pressure Index for every past game For each historical game of the player:


Pressure Score = 
  (100 - Team Possession %) × 0.6 
  + High-Press Opponent Flag (0 or 1) × 40

•  High-Press Opponent Flag: You can hard-code a list of known high-pressing teams or calculate it from average opponent PPDA / pressures if available. Even a simple list of aggressive teams works well at the start.
2. Split the player’s games into two buckets
•  High Pressure games (Pressure Score > 55–60)
•  Low/Normal Pressure games (Pressure Score < 45)
3. Calculate two key numbers
•  Average Passes in High Pressure games
•  Average Passes in Low/Normal Pressure games
4. Create the Pressure Multiplier

Pressure Multiplier = High Pressure Avg Passes / Low Pressure Avg Passes

Interpretation:
•  Multiplier < 0.85 → Pressure Sensitive (Freezer)
•  Multiplier 0.85 – 1.10 → Neutral
•  Multiplier > 1.10 → Pressure Resistant / Thrives under pressure
5. Apply it in live predictions When projecting today’s game:
•  Estimate today’s expected possession + opponent press intensity
•  Apply the player’s personal Pressure Multiplier to the base projection
Extra Smart Rules to Add
•  Minimum sample size: Only classify a player if they have at least 6–8 games in each bucket.
•  Weight recent seasons higher.
•  Centre-backs and defensive midfielders often show clearer patterns than attacking players.
•  Some players only freeze against elite high-press teams (you can create a second, stricter flag).
Simple Version You Can Launch Fast
Even without a full High-Press flag, you can start with just possession:
•  Games where his team had < 45% possession → High Pressure
•  Games where his team had > 55% possession → Low Pressure
This already catches a large part of the “freezes under pressure” effect and works with pure API-Football data.
