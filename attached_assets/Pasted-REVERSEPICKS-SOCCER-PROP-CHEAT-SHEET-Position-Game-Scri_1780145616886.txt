CORE PRINCIPLES
•  Default Edge: UNDER dominates pass_attempts and shots. OVER stronger on tackles and dribbles.
•  Script Priority: Possession > Result > Scoring Scenario.
•  Home Advantage: Stronger UNDER on pass_attempts (especially RW/RB).
•  Away Edge: Stronger OVER on tackles (CDM/RB) and dribbles (LW/CF).
