What I recommend building

I would not start by simply adding more API endpoints or asking the AI to write longer paragraphs. That could make the prose longer without making it smarter.

I recommend building a structured tactical reasoning layer with five sections.

1. Match script

First explain the likely overall game:

Which team is favored?
Is the player’s team expected to have more or less possession?
Is the match likely to be controlled, open, transitional, or defensive?
Is the team likely to chase the match or protect a lead?
Are there knockout/aggregate stakes?
Is there a meaningful venue or home/away effect?
Example:

Match script: The market expects Liverpool to control the match at home. That points toward more settled possession and repeated attacking sequences rather than a purely transitional game.
2. Player role

Then explain what the player’s role means for the specific prop:

Player role: Bruno Fernandes is being treated as an advanced creator rather than a normal central midfielder. His pass projection depends less on raw team possession and more on whether he remains involved in build-up or receives the ball higher between the lines.
That is much better than just displaying “MID · Advanced Playmaker.”

3. Opponent interaction

Then connect the opponent to the player:

Does the opponent press high?
Does the opponent defend deep?
Does the opponent allow central circulation?
Does the opponent force wide buildup?
Does the opponent concede volume to this position?
Is the matchup likely to increase or decrease the player’s involvement?
Example:

Opponent interaction: The opponent’s expected shape leaves central circulation available but limits clean final-third access. That supports short and medium passing volume, while making shots and final actions less reliable.
4. Prop mechanism

This should explicitly answer why the requested stat could go over or under:

Pass mechanism: The OVER case depends on the player remaining involved in first- and second-phase possession. The UNDER case becomes more likely if the opponent’s press forces the team to bypass midfield with direct balls.
Or:

Tackle mechanism: The UNDER is supported because the player’s team is expected to control possession, reducing the amount of defensive work reaching his zone. The opponent’s attack profile also does not create a strong tackle pathway for this position.
5. Evidence and uncertainty

Every analysis should state:

number of relevant recent matches
whether lineup is confirmed or projected
whether possession is verified or estimated
whether opponent positional samples are sufficient
whether the player’s role is confirmed or inferred
what information is missing
Example:

Evidence quality: The role and player history are well supported, but the opponent positional sample is thin and the lineup is not confirmed. This is a moderate tactical read, not a high-certainty matchup signal.
What the final paragraph should sound like

Instead of a generic paragraph like:

“The player has good passing numbers and the team has strong possession, so the OVER is supported.”
The app should produce something closer to:

Tactical read: This is primarily a possession-involvement angle, not a pure player-form angle. The player is expected to operate as a deeper central midfielder, where his pass volume is driven by repeated circulation during settled attacks. His team is favored and projects to control more of the ball, which supports the number of possession sequences available to him. The opponent’s shape may allow central recycling but could reduce clean progression into the final third. That favors pass volume more than shots or key attacking actions. The read is supported by his recent role and pass history, but the lineup is not fully confirmed, so the edge should be treated as moderate.
That sounds like football analysis because it explains:

role
team behavior
opponent behavior
match script
prop-specific mechanism
uncertainty
What should happen in the Player Profile

The Player Profile should not just be:

hit rate
past picks
per-90 stats
It should include a compact section like:

Player tactical identity

Primary role
Common position
Passing/attacking/defensive profile
Whether production rises with possession
Whether production rises when the team is under pressure
Typical involvement level
Example:

Tactical identity: High-involvement central creator. Passing output is strongest when his team controls possession and he remains involved in buildup. Shot volume is more dependent on late arrivals and set-piece responsibility than on possession alone.
Then show the numbers underneath as evidence.

What should not be done

I would avoid:

Asking Gemini to freely invent tactical explanations from a giant raw JSON blob.
Displaying every API-Football number and calling that “analysis.”
Claiming exact zones or marking assignments that API-Football does not verify.
Letting possession automatically imply every player prop goes OVER.
Treating formation labels as proof of actual in-game positioning.
Making the tactical layer change projections before it is validated.
Using more verbose paragraphs as a substitute for better reasoning.
My recommendation

The right direction is:

Keep API-Football as the data source.
Build a structured, player-specific tactical packet from the verified fixture.
Improve role classification beyond broad position groups.
Add explicit opponent interaction and prop mechanisms.
Separate verified data from inference and unavailable information.
Generate the final explanation from that structured packet.
Redesign the analysis and Player Profile sections around a football narrative.
Keep tactical signals shadow-only until walk-forward validation proves they improve predictions.
So yes, this can be fixed, but I would describe the required work as:

“Upgrade the tactical analysis from a collection of statistical evidence cards into a verified, role-aware, matchup-specific football reasoning system. It must explain the match script, player role, opponent interaction, prop mechanism, and evidence limitations without inventing unavailable positional data.”
That is the direction I recommend before building.
